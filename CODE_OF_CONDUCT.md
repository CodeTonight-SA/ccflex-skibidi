# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a place to celebrate work and have fun doing it. Brag about
your numbers, not at other people's expense.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of skill, background, or how big
  their token count is.
- Keep the humour aimed at the situation, never at a person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming someone gamed the board — the maths
  is public; check it first.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sharing others' private data. Only your own numbers, only what you commit.
- Deliberate attempts to falsify entries or subvert verification.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove entries, edit or reject contributions, and ban
participants for behaviour that violates this code. Report concerns privately
to the maintainer via the contact in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
